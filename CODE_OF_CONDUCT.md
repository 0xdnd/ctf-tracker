# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This is a short summary in our own words; the Covenant is the reference text.

## Our pledge

We want participation in ZeroBox to be a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and own your mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling, or other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through GitHub (a private report on this repository, or a direct message to the maintainer, [@0xdnd](https://github.com/0xdnd)). Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, keep reporters' details confidential, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or ban anyone who breaks these standards.
