An offline pentest notebook: your notes stay in your browser

The tracker app has no account, sends no telemetry and has no server holding your data.

The source is public on GitHub, so you can check what it stores and what it sends.

Stored in
LocalStorage and IndexedDB on your device. Clearing site data erases it.
Backups
JSON export and restore, with optional passphrase encryption.
AI scan interpretation (optional)
Off by default. Bring your own key. Data is sent only when you press send, and only to api.anthropic.com.

Free CTF tools, cheatsheets and guides
